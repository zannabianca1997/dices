`dices` provides built-in operators to manipulate values:

- [The `d` operator][4.1. Throwing], the most basic functionality of `dices`.
- [Arithmetic operators][4.2. Arithmetic] such as `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`,
  `%`.
- [The `~` operator][4.3. Joining] concatenates lists, merges maps, and joins
  strings via the `~` operator.
- [The `^` operator][4.4. Repeat] evaluates an expression multiple time.
- [Filter operators][4.5. Filters] keep or remove the highest or lowest
  elements from lists.
