A set of operators is provided to manipulate values.

- _4.1. Throwing_ the `d` operator, the most basic functionality of `dices`.
- _4.2. Arithmetic operators_ such as `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `%`.
- _4.3. Joining_ concatenates lists, merges maps, and joins strings via the `~` operator.
- _4.4. Repeat_ evaluates an expression multiple times with `^`.
- _4.5. Filter operators_ keep or remove the highest or lowest elements from lists.
