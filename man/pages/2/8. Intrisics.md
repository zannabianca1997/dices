Injected values are values that comes from the environment, provided to
give access to additional capability. 

The most basic injected can be accessed through the std library, accessed from
`std`. Additionally, some injected are available by default into the
environment. The list is available at `std.prelude`.

```
>>> std
<|prelude: <|...|>, ...|>
>>> std.prelude
<|...|>
```

The documentation of `std` is available in the manual at the topic _5. Standard Library_.
